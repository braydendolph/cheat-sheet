# Core
## Mouse
Front btn | Select all Town Centers
Back btn | Go to Next Idle Villager
Middle | Go to Last Notification

## Hands on keys
Space | Select all Visible Military
Caps | Go to Next Idle Military Unit
` | Go to Selected Object
H | Go to Town Center
Tab | Attack Move
F1 | Delete Unit
Y | Set Gather Point on Self

## Group Commands
Ctrl+1–0 | Create Group
1–0 | Select Group (×2 jump)
Shift+1–0 | Append Group
Ctrl+Shift+1–0 | Select & Center Group

## Town Center
Q | Villager (Shift ×5)
A | Loom
S | Wheelbarrow, Hand Cart
D | Town Watch, Town Patrol
Z | Age Up
B | Ring Town Bell
F | Go Back to Work
