# Core
## Mouse
Front btn | All Town Centers
Back btn | Idle villager
Middle | Last alert

## Always
Space | Visible army
Tab | Attack move
Caps | Idle military
` | Go to selected
F1 | Delete
Y | Gather on self

## Groups
Ctrl+1–5 | Save
1–5 | Select (×2 jump)
Shift+1–5 | Add

## Town Center
Q | Villager (Shift ×5)
A | Loom
S | Wheelbarrow
D | Town Watch
Z | Age up
B | Town bell
